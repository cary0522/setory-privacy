Privacy Policy

01

Introduction

Setory is a personal workout tracking app designed with an offline-first approach. This Privacy Policy explains how Setory handles information that you provide, create, or access through device features while using the app.

Core workout records are primarily processed and stored on your device. Setory does not automatically upload this information to a Setory-owned cloud database merely because you use the basic recording features. However, the free version integrates Google AdMob, so the app may exchange necessary information with an advertising service provider. See “Advertising and Third-party Services” for details.

02

Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Setory may process the following information on your device:

  • Your display name, language, and other app preferences.
  • Exercises, muscle groups, equipment, and workout templates.
  • Workout dates and times, weights, repetitions, sets, completion status, and history.
  • Equipment photos that you actively take or select, and personal photos that you choose as sharing backgrounds.
  • Times and related settings for local workout reminders.

Core workout-recording features currently do not require an online account. We have no confirmed cloud synchronization or cloud backup service for these features.

03

Local Data Storage

Core workout data is primarily stored in a local database on your device so that Setory can create, edit, and display your records on the device. This information is not automatically uploaded to a Setory-owned cloud database when you use the basic workout-recording features.

Device backups, restores, and other system-level retention mechanisms may handle data according to Apple’s systems and your backup settings. Setory does not control those system-level mechanisms.

04

Camera and Photos

When you actively use the equipment-photo features, Setory may request camera or photo-library access so that you can take a photo or select an equipment photo, and associate it with equipment.

These features are initiated by your actions. Setory does not automatically scan your entire photo library. Related photos are primarily handled through local device storage.

You can manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. Declining a permission may prevent the related features from working, but does not affect features that do not require that permission.

05

Advertising and Third-party Services

The free version of Setory integrates Google AdMob to provide advertising. Setory uses the @capacitor-community/admob SDK, and the free and Pro versions may have different advertising display logic.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, ad interaction information, identifiers, or diagnostic data based on the SDK configuration, your consent choices, and applicable law. Ad personalization and related tracking behavior depend on the actual SDK configuration, your choices, and applicable requirements. Based on the information currently confirmed, we do not state here that Setory necessarily conducts cross-app tracking, necessarily uses IDFA, or has completed an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) consent flow.

Google’s handling of relevant information may be governed by its privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

We have no confirmed integration of Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google Analytics at this time. If analytics or monitoring services are added in the future, we will update this policy as appropriate.

06

Notifications

Setory uses iOS local notifications to provide workout reminders. You set the reminder times and related preferences in the app, while iOS manages notification permissions.

We have no confirmed use of a remote push-notification service. You can manage notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

07

Sharing Features

Setory can create workout-record images on your device. You can choose different sharing styles and may select a personal photo as a sharing background.

Only after you actively choose a sharing destination may the content be handled by iOS sharing features and the third-party app you select. Third-party platforms may handle your shared content under their own privacy policies; after you share it, the information is no longer fully controlled by Setory.

08

Data Retention and Deletion

You can manage or delete some workout data using the features provided by Setory. Uninstalling the app will usually remove local data stored by the app on your device, but iOS backups and other system-level retention mechanisms may affect what remains.

Information handled by an advertising service provider may not be deleted at the same time as you uninstall Setory. To learn about or manage information that Google may retain, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) and the tools it provides.

09

Children’s Privacy

Setory is not directed primarily to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through Setory. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us using the details below.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features, third-party services, legal requirements, or actual data practices change. We will publish updated content and an updated effective date on this page. Your continued use of Setory means that you have read the updated policy.
