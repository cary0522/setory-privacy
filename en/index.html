SETORY · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Setory handles workout records, photos, notifications, sharing content, and advertising services.

Product: Setory Effective date: October 22, 2026

01

Introduction

Setory is a personal workout tracking app designed with an offline-first approach. This Privacy Policy explains how Setory handles information that you provide, create, or access through device features while using the app.

Core workout records are primarily processed and stored on your device. Setory does not automatically upload this information to a Setory-owned cloud database merely because you use the basic recording features. However, the free version integrates Google AdMob, so the app may exchange necessary information with an advertising service provider. See “Advertising and Third-party Services” for details.

02

Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Setory may process the following information on your device:

  • Your display name, language, and other app preferences.
  • Exercises, muscle groups, equipment, and workout templates.
  • Workout dates and times, weights, repetitions, sets, completion status, and history.
  • Equipment photos that you actively take or select, and personal photos that you choose as sharing backgrounds.
  • Times and related settings for local workout reminders.

Core workout-recording features do not require an online account. Setory currently does not provide account-based cloud synchronization or cloud backup.

03

Local Data Storage

Core workout data is primarily stored in SQLite on your device so that Setory can create, edit, and display your records on the device. This information is not automatically uploaded to a Setory-owned cloud database when you use the basic workout-recording features.

Device backups, restores, and other system-level retention mechanisms may handle data according to Apple’s systems and your backup settings. Setory does not control those system-level mechanisms.

04

Camera and Photos

When you actively use the equipment-photo or sharing-background features, Setory uses the camera or a system-provided photo selection feature according to your actions. You can take an equipment photo, select one from your photo library, or choose a personal photo as a sharing background.

These features are initiated by your actions. Setory does not automatically scan or freely access your entire photo library. The photos Setory can access depend on the system-provided selection method and your authorization. Equipment photos are primarily stored on your device for equipment identification, while a sharing-background photo is used only for the current sharing flow.

You can manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings. Declining a permission may prevent the related features from working, but does not affect features that do not require that permission.

05

Advertising and Third-party Services

The free version of Setory may display Google AdMob ads. Pro benefits include removing ads within the app. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, and information related to ad delivery or measurement based on their service settings, your choices, and applicable law.

Google’s handling of relevant information may be governed by its privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab).

06

Notifications

Setory uses iOS local notifications for workout reminders and existing in-app reminders such as rest intervals. You set the reminder times and related preferences in the app, while iOS manages notification permissions.

You can manage notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

07

Sharing Features

Setory can create workout-record images on your device. You can choose different sharing styles and may actively select a personal photo as a sharing background; that photo is used only for the current sharing flow.

Only after you actively choose a sharing destination may the content be handled by iOS sharing features and the third-party app you select. Third-party platforms may handle your shared content under their own privacy policies; after you share it, the information is no longer fully controlled by Setory.

08

Data Retention and Deletion

You can use Setory’s features to delete individual workout records or manage other data you create. You can also use the app’s local data management feature to clear data stored on your device. Data cleared in this way may not be recoverable; please review your selection before proceeding. Uninstalling the app will usually remove local data stored by the app on your device, but iOS backups and other system-level retention mechanisms may affect what remains.

Information handled by an advertising service provider may not be deleted at the same time as you uninstall Setory. To learn about or manage information that Google may retain, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) and the tools it provides.

09

Children’s Privacy

Setory is not directed primarily to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through Setory. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us using the details below.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Setory’s features, third-party services, or data practices change. We will publish the latest complete policy and effective date on the official privacy policy website. The app provides a policy summary and a link to the complete policy, but its text may not be updated immediately after the website changes. We recommend checking the official website periodically for the latest version.
