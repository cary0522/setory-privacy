When Reporting a Problem

If you encountered an issue, please include:

  • The feature involved, such as workout records, templates, history, photos, notifications, or sharing.
  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and Setory version.
  • The steps that reproduce the issue and the result you expected.
  • A screenshot when useful, as long as it does not contain sensitive information.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

03

Quick Help

  • Workout records: Core records are primarily stored on the current device. Check before uninstalling Setory or clearing its data.
  • Photos and camera: If equipment photos or sharing backgrounds do not work, check Setory’s photo and camera permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Reminders and rest notifications: Check Setory’s notification permission and reminder settings, and make sure the device is not restricting notifications.
  • Sharing: After completing a workout, you can choose a style and destination in the sharing flow.
